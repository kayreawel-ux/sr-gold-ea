How the EA works

  • Calculates a recent support level from the lowest low and resistance from the highest high.
  • Waits for a fully closed candle to show a rejection pattern near a level.
  • Places a trade only if trading is allowed, spread is within the configured limit and no matching EA position is open.
  • Sets a stop-loss beyond the signal candle and a take-profit based on the selected reward-to-risk ratio.

Broker symbol names, contract specifications, minimum volume and spread differ. Settings must be checked on your own demo account.
